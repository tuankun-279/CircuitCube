Add group

A new group starts hidden. Give it shapes under Auto levels, wait for (or run) a monthly batch, then enable it here.

Levels are made by AI only. On day 1 of each month (08:56 Vietnam time) a scheduled task generates each enabled group's new levels and its weekly challenge sets from the settings below, checks and play-tests every one, and queues them. Every Thursday at 07:06 a share of the queue is released and published, so players get new levels each week without an app update.
